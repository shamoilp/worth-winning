Part 3 · Score it, or look back

WIWI: the ROI of Ego

Your WIWI score is one number that says whether a win is worth chasing. It weighs the realistic payoff against everything the deal costs you, including the ego tax: the extra you'd pay just to avoid backing down.

Why “WIWI”? It's D. Murali's tongue-in-cheek “Corporate Latin”, a nod to Caesar's vici (“I conquered”). The score asks whether the conquest is worth it.

WIWI = Value × Chance Direct cost + Opportunity cost + Ego tax

  • Below 1.0 walk away
  • 1.0 – 1.5 thin, set limits
  • 1.5+ worth pursuing
